# Abby Covert Principles for Product IA

Core ideas drawn from Abby Covert’s work on making sense of products and messes.

## Information Architecture Definition
Information architecture is the way we arrange the parts of something to make sense as a whole. It always exists. The question is whether it is intentional.

## Three Core Lenses
- **Ontology** — What exists? What are the core entities and how do they relate?
- **Taxonomy** — How do we group and order those entities?
- **Choreography** — How do people move through and experience the structure?

## Controlled Vocabulary
Inconsistent language is one of the highest-frequency failure modes in products.  
Create and maintain a controlled vocabulary of the key terms used across the product (navigation, buttons, empty states, errors, documentation).

Without consistent meaning, users (and teams) start believing different things exist when only the labels differ.

## Key Questions for Any Product
- What terms and concepts do you use to deliver the product experience?
- What organizational structures do you use to present those terms and concepts?
- To what degree is the meaning you intend clear to the people for whom you intended it?

## Practical Stance
There is no single “right” structure. There are structures that better support the intended users and jobs. Prefer clarity and consistency over novelty.
